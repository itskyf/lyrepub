# TTS candidate survey — September 2026

**Purpose.** A small set of directly usable checkpoints for Issue [#13](https://github.com/itskyf/lyrepub/issues/13), before the final TTS experiment is frozen. This is a documentation survey, not model selection or experimental evidence. Follow [source characterization](source-characterization.md) for the actual material and [protocol](protocol.md) for the research contract. Vietnamese narration is mandatory; names, numbers/dates, punctuation and dialogue, longer passages, and limited foreign-language/proper-name material are the relevant challenges. No training, fine-tuning, architecture work, service infrastructure, or exhaustive ranking is proposed.

**Evidence notation.** **D** = capability/interface documented by the model author or official implementation, **A** = author demonstration, test, or qualitative/benchmark claim; **P** = behavior and suitability still to be established by our own pilot. D does not imply that Vietnamese quality, robust long-form output, or cross-language behavior has been independently verified here. This survey used public cards, repositories, licenses, and inference documentation available by 27 September 2026; no weights were run or audio evaluated.

## Relevance and pilot decisions

| Capability group | Relevance to this coursework |
| --- | --- |
| Vietnamese intelligibility; treatment of names, numbers/dates, punctuation/dialogue and sentence boundaries | **Required to inspect.** Documented language lists alone cannot establish pronunciation or natural narration. Use the existing EDA's representative and challenge material, without copying it here. |
| Longer passages, stable voice across chunks, reproducible normalization and segmentation | **Required to inspect** for audiobook feasibility. Record source text, text processing, voice/reference, seed if supported, checkpoint/runtime revision, generated segments and any retry or failure. |
| English/French/proper names, code-switching and cross-lingual voice continuity | **Pilot-dependent.** The source has limited foreign-language material. Test only where the selected EDA cases actually require it; model language support is not proof of in-sentence switching. |
| Presets, reference cloning, voice design, emotion/prosody and speaker continuity | **Potentially useful.** Choose a reproducible narration voice and inspect whether any conditioning improves the source, without making expressive controls a new requirement. Check reference rights. |
| Non-verbal sounds, singing, extensive emotion tags, multi-speaker production, live streaming, serving and editing | **Not a project requirement.** Dialogue punctuation may matter, but does not itself require character voices, laughter generation, songs, real-time service, or audio editing. |

For the **minimum feasibility pass**, take one ordinary Vietnamese narration span and the existing EDA challenge spans that jointly cover a name/proper name, number or date, punctuation/dialogue, a longer passage/chunk boundary, and the limited foreign-language case if present. Reuse exactly the relevant existing text rather than inventing a new benchmark. Generate enough audio to hear failures and transitions, then have the repository owner perform focused qualitative listening. This is **not** a MOS study, a scored comparison, or #14's final experiment; no benchmark, weights, routing, or configuration is frozen by this document.

The first-pass shortlist compares two materially different Vietnamese-first local paths (VieNeu and ZeroTTS), a multilingual path for the limited foreign-language material (VoxCPM2), and a dedicated Vietnamese OmniVoice adaptation (G-OmniVoice) only if its gated access and effective dependency/license terms are resolved. If that gate or license chain blocks the pilot, use public KhanhTTS-OmniVoice for the OmniVoice-family check rather than adding another arm. Higgs remains a reserve: its expressive surface is documented, but those features are not a source-derived requirement and do not by themselves justify its larger runtime footprint. There is **no evidence yet that multiple TTS models are necessary**: a single adequate checkpoint is the simpler outcome. If pilot evidence later justifies multiple models for particular source cases, specify deterministic, documented routing from observable text features and freeze it before #14.

Other Vietnamese-ready checkpoints remain plausible but do not add a distinct first-pass question: [ZipVoice-Vietnamese-2500h](https://huggingface.co/hynt/ZipVoice-Vietnamese-2500h) and [F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice](https://huggingface.co/hynt/F5-TTS-Vietnamese-ViVoice) are noncommercial/share-alike adaptations with reference-cloning workflows; the latter needs explicit longer-text chunking. Their availability and terms should be checked again if promoted. The original [Qwen3-TTS](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base), [IndexTTS-2.5](https://huggingface.co/IndexTeam/IndexTTS-2.5), and [Voxtral 4B TTS](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-4B-TTS-2603) do not document Vietnamese for those original checkpoints; an adapted checkpoint must be evaluated on its own. Hosted TTS APIs may be directly usable but add account, service, and reproducibility dependencies, so they are not needed for this first local feasibility set.

**Open checks for #13:** actual access and effective license chain for G-OmniVoice/KhanhTTS; Higgs hardware fit and license conditions if promoted; audio behavior on Vietnamese numbers, names, dialogue, chunk seams and foreign names for each runnable pilot; whether vendor-demonstrated emotions, code-switching, or long-form claims help this source. No author WER, similarity, MOS, speed, or memory figure above is a result of this project.
